# Test Brand: Maison Soleil Bridal

## Brand Overview
**Name:** Maison Soleil Bridal
**Tagline:** "Your Gown Should Know You Before You Walk Down the Aisle"
**Location:** Miami, FL (Design District atelier)
**Founded:** 2024
**Founder:** Isabella Reyes — third-generation seamstress, trained in Milan, raised in Miami

## What They Do
Maison Soleil creates fully custom, handmade bridal gowns. No off-the-rack, no alterations to existing designs. Every gown is built from scratch — from the first sketch to the final stitch — around the bride's body, personality, and wedding vision.

What makes them different: they don't just measure you and ask what silhouette you want. They run a full "Bridal Identity" consultation where they study the bride's aesthetic across her life — how she decorates her home, what she wears daily, her Pinterest boards, her venue choice, even her partner's style — to design a gown that feels like HER, even if she can't articulate what that means yet.

## Services
1. **The Full Soleil Experience** — Complete bridal design journey (6-9 months). Includes Bridal Identity consultation, 3D design preview, 4-6 fittings, handmade gown, custom veil or accessories.
2. **The Soleil Suite** — Gown + coordinated bridal party dresses (matching aesthetic, not matching dresses). For brides who want the full wedding look to feel cohesive.
3. **Heirloom Reimagined** — Transform a family heirloom gown (mother's, grandmother's) into a modern design that honors the original while fitting the bride's vision.

## Target Audience
- Brides aged 28-42
- Budget: $8,000-$35,000 per gown
- Values craftsmanship over brand names
- Wants something no one else will ever wear
- Often frustrated by the bridal industry's one-size-fits-all approach
- Miami, South Florida, and destination brides

## Brand Voice
- Intimate and knowing — like a close friend who happens to be brilliant at design
- Sensory language — textures, light, movement, how fabric falls
- Confident but never pushy — the gown speaks for itself
- Cultural warmth — Miami's Latin influence, family, celebration, joy
- Never clinical or corporate — this is art, not retail
